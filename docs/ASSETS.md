## Interface icons

General interface icons use [Lucide](https://lucide.dev/), sourced from the [official icon repository](https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/main/icons). The ISC license and Feather-derived MIT notice are included at `public/assets/icons/LICENSE-Lucide.txt`. Icons share a 24 × 24 grid and 2 px stroke; brand icons retain their own visual identities. No platform-rendered emoji are used.
